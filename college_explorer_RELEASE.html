Tuition, cost, and merit figures are estimates for planning purposes only — verify against each school's own Net Price Calculator. SAT ranges reflect the middle 50% of each school's admitted class. In-Texas schools use in-state tuition; all others use nonresident/out-of-state tuition. Region groups Northeast/Mid-Atlantic → East, Pacific/Mountain → West, Gulf/Southeast/Texas → South, and Midwest → North.

Ratings (Engineering Rating, ME/AE Rating, IE/SE Rating, Academic Balance, Culture – High Energy, Culture – Supportive, Alumni Network, Public Brand) are scored 0–100 and are qualitative judgment calls, not sourced data — every one of the 29 schools in this dataset carries a full set of ratings. Reasonable people would score these differently.

Engineering Rating reflects the pedigree of the school's overall engineering school — a blend of reputation and the economic/career outcomes of its graduates. ME/AE Rating and IE/SE Rating apply that same pedigree lens specifically to the Mechanical/Aerospace and Industrial/Systems programs, since those are Colin's two areas of interest — a school can score well on overall Engineering Rating while scoring lower on one of these if that particular department is thin or nonexistent.

Culture – High Energy rates the level of energy, enthusiasm, and stimulation on campus (big, spirited programs like Texas A&M and Auburn score highest; quieter, low-key campuses score lowest). Culture – Supportive rates how collaborative and teamwork-oriented the culture is, versus cutthroat, competitive, or transactional. These are independent dimensions — a school can be high-energy without being especially supportive, or vice versa.

Overall Fit and Mismatch Watch (shown only in the Top 5 cards, never as table columns) are short written summaries of why a school could work for Colin and where the real friction points are, respectively — populated for all 29 schools, so any school can appear in the Top 5. The Top 5 cards also show program-level notes on ME/AE and IE/SE pedigree, each with links to the official department or program page where one exists.

Fit Rating = weighted average of ME/AE Rating (20%), IE/SE Rating (20%), Engineering Rating (15%), Academic Balance (10%), Culture – High Energy (10%), Culture – Supportive (10%), Alumni Network (5%), and Public Brand (10%). These weights are fixed — see Settings for a read-only breakdown. All 29 schools are scored. This is the default sort.
